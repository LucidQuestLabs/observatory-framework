# Safety and Ethics

## Core Rules

- Be evidence-led.
- Avoid unsupported accusations.
- Protect private individuals.
- Preserve source context.
- Mark uncertainty clearly.
- Keep advocacy separate from evidence.
- Maintain a visible correction path.

## Legal Caution

Do not state that a company, official, or person committed wrongdoing unless a reliable source directly establishes that fact and human legal/editorial review approves the language.

Prefer language such as:

- The filing states...
- The record indicates...
- According to the agenda...
- This raises a question about...
- Further confirmation is needed...

Avoid conclusory or inflammatory claims unless legally established and reviewed.

## Privacy

Do not publish home addresses of private individuals, personal phone numbers, personal emails, medical information, unverified allegations, or harassment-prone details.
