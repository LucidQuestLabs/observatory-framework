- City council agendas.
- County board minutes.
- Zoning applications.
- Conditional use permits.
- Building permits.
- Tax incentive agreements.
- Annexation records.
- Public hearing notices.

- Integrated resource plans.
- Substation plans.
- Transmission planning.

- Wastewater treatment capacity plans.
- Cooling water allocations.
- Emergency generator permits.

- Air permits.

- Environmental assessments.
- Stormwater permits.
- Wetlands or habitat reviews.
- Noise studies.
- Heat island considerations.
- Water stress maps.

- Company announcements.
- SEC filings.
- Investor presentations.
- Sustainability reports.
- Tax abatement records.
- Bond disclosures.

- Local news.
- Community meeting transcripts.
- Public comments.
- Civic group reports.
- Social media leads.
- Trade press.

- GIS maps.
- Satellite imagery.
- FEMA flood maps.
- Land use maps.
