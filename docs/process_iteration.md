# Process Iteration

Observatory Framework should improve through disciplined repetition. Alongside evidence ledgers, the project maintains a snag loop for operational learning.

## Snag Loop

```text
Snag -> Triage -> Workaround -> Next Action -> Lesson -> SOP Update
```

## Why This Matters

Investigations rarely fail only because of missing facts. They also get slowed by unclear schemas, awkward review handoffs, inaccessible sources, fragile scripts, deployment assumptions, and ambiguity about who can approve what. Snag logging makes that friction visible.

## Cadence

- During active work: log snags when they affect evidence quality, review speed, publication safety, or repeatability.
- End of target cycle: review open snags before creating the dossier.
- End of week: fold repeated snags into SOP, schema, template, or script updates.

## Useful Snag Categories

- `source`: access, citation, archival, OCR, or provenance problem.
- `schema`: missing field, confusing enum, validation issue.
- `agent`: prompt, handoff, role, or output quality issue.
- `review`: approval, rejection, clarification, or escalation friction.
- `ledger`: append-only, correction, merge, or ID issue.
- `report`: dossier, briefing, map, or public summary issue.
- `deployment`: static site, Netlify, export, or environment issue.
- `ethics`: privacy, legal caution, publication risk, or redaction issue.
- `process`: coordination, cadence, ownership, or repeatability issue.
