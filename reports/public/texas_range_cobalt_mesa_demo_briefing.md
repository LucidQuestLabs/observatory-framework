# Public Demo Briefing: Texas Range / Cobalt Mesa Campus

Synthetic demonstration data. Not a real source-backed claim.

This public demo shows how Observatory Framework will handle a Texas Range data center investigation once real sources are selected. It uses fictional sources and fictional observations to demonstrate method, not to make claims about any real facility, agency, company, or community.

## Demonstrated Workflow

1. Register sources.
2. Extract atomic observations.
3. Assign confidence and interpretation level.
4. Route observations through review.
5. Append approved observations to a ledger.
6. Generate a mini-dossier and public briefing.

## Demo Target

- Name: Cobalt Mesa Compute Campus
- Geography: Fictional Mesa Verde County, Texas
- Status: Proposed / conceptual
- Use case: Texas Range first-usecase prototype

## Demo Findings

- Faux records identify planning, water, electric, and air-review surfaces.
- Approved observations preserve uncertainty and avoid final claims.
- Pending observations model gaps around tax incentives, legal review, ownership, and community comments.
