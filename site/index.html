Evidence-led infrastructure intelligence

Source-backed observations for public-interest research.

A human-reviewed framework for collecting, preserving, analyzing, and publishing structured observations about AI data centers and other complex infrastructure systems.

Core Loop

  1. Source

    Register primary and contextual evidence before extracting claims.

  2. Observation

    Create atomic, source-backed records with confidence and interpretation labels.

  3. Review

    Human reviewers approve, reject, clarify, restrict, or escalate.

  4. Ledger

    Approved observations are appended to durable JSONL ledgers.

  5. Dossier

    Reports and briefings are generated from reviewed observations.

Baseline Corpus

The corpus includes SOPs, schemas, prompts, templates, review checklists, ledgers, target workspaces, and documentation. It is plain-file-first so agents and humans can inspect every step.

Deployment

This static surface can be deployed with Netlify using the included netlify.toml. Public reports should be exported here only after review and redaction.
