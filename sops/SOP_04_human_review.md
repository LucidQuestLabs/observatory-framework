# SOP 04: Human Review

## Purpose

Give humans a simple, auditable process for deciding what enters the approved ledger.

## Reviewer Decisions

- `approved`: Sufficiently supported for ledger entry.
- `rejected`: Unsupported, inaccurate, irrelevant, or inappropriate.
- `needs_clarification`: More source detail, narrower wording, or follow-up needed.
- `superseded`: Replaced by a later record while preserving history.
- `escalated`: Requires legal, policy, domain, or publication review.
- `restricted`: Supported but not suitable for public release.

## Checklist

- Sources are valid and traceable.
- Observations are atomic.
- Source locations are sufficient.
- Confidence levels are appropriate.
- Interpretation level is accurate.
- Language is not overstated.
- Privacy and publication sensitivity are reviewed.
- Open questions and contradictions are captured.

## Output

Use `templates/review_decision_template.md` or a review packet comment.
