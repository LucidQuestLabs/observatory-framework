# SOP 06: Report Generation

## Purpose

Generate briefings, dossiers, maps, and public/internal outputs from reviewed observations.

## Rules

- Use approved observations by default.
- Clearly mark draft/internal reports that include pending observations.
- Preserve source mapping.
- Separate findings, interpretation, contradictions, questions, and recommendations.
- Avoid overstating low-confidence material.

## Report Types

- Mini-dossier.
- Issue briefing.
- Source index.
- Timeline.
- Entity map.
- Public summary.
- Internal memo.

## Mini-Dossier Structure

1. Executive summary.
2. Target profile.
3. Key findings.
4. Timeline.
5. Entities involved.
6. Facility or site details.
7. Water observations.
8. Energy observations.
9. Land use and permitting.
10. Environmental and community considerations.
11. Incentives and finance.
12. Contradictions and gaps.
13. Open questions.
14. Recommended next actions.
15. Source index.
16. Appendix: approved observations.
