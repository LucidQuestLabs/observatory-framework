# SOP 07: Redaction and Publication

## Purpose

Prepare evidence-led outputs for appropriate public, restricted, private, or embargoed distribution.

## Rules

- Do not publish private personal information.
- Redact sensitive source details when required.
- Keep publication status on observations and sources.
- Preserve internal source mapping even when public outputs are redacted.
- Do not publish serious allegations without review.

## Publication Statuses

- `internal`: usable inside the working corpus.
- `public_ready`: approved for public-facing output.
- `redacted`: public version requires omissions or softened details.
- `embargoed`: do not publish until condition is met.
