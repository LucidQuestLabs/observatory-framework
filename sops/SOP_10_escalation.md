# SOP 10: Escalation

## Purpose

Route sensitive or uncertain material to the right human review path.

## Escalate When

- A claim implies wrongdoing.
- Private individuals may be affected.
- Sources conflict materially.
- Legal, regulatory, or policy expertise is needed.
- Publication may create reputational, safety, privacy, or contractual risk.
- Restricted or embargoed sources are involved.

## Escalation Destinations

- Human lead reviewer.
- Legal/editorial reviewer.
- Domain expert.
- Data steward.
- Publication/redaction editor.
- Client or partner reviewer when applicable.
